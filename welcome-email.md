Subject: Welcome to EcoExposure

Welcome to EcoExposure. Your Ecotera Asia account is ready to use.

You can collect samples, record field data, and review your saved records.

To get started, log in to Ecotera Asia and select New Sample from the Home screen. To record a field observation, select Field Data. You can also try Practice before collecting your first sample.

If you are participating through a project or organization, follow any additional instructions from your project lead.

Thank you for contributing to EcoExposure.
