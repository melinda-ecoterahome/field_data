How New Sample works

This demo does not require a kit code or sign-in.

  1. Add sample details and optional GPS.
  2. Tap any Take Photo button to see two clear-water samples, the reagent step, then the after-reaction comparison and table.
  3. Return to New Sample or Home.

The comparison is a fixed example. It does not analyze your water, camera image, notes, or GPS. Details are not uploaded or saved.
