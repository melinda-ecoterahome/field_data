How New Sample works

Use this page to save an EcoExposure sample with a registered kit.

DO NOT STIR, SWIRL, OR MIX

Zero-shear matters: mixing disrupts the assay. If powder clumps remain at the surface, gently poke only those surface clumps. Do not stir or mix the liquid.

  1. Verify a kit. Scan inside the app or enter the printed manual code. Wait for the verified message.
  2. Match the stage. Use PRE before reagent or POST after reaction, following your kit instructions for preparation and timing.
  3. Add details. Enter your name and sample label. Add notes and location; use the same label for matching PRE/POST observations.
  4. Take photos. Use the in-app camera and check the preview. For shadows or uneven lighting, pop open the photo box. At least one photo is required.
  5. Save and review. Wait for confirmation, then check My Data. Each successful save uses one submission from that code.

For notes or a field photo without a kit, use Field. To rehearse without saving scientific data or using a kit submission, use Practice.
