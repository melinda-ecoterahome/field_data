Phosphate

Field screening for nutrient loading and conditions associated with algal-bloom risk.

Why this is useful

Excess phosphate can contribute to eutrophication and harmful algal blooms, threatening aquatic ecosystems and potentially affecting irrigation, crops, livestock and community water uses.

Laboratory analysis provides the most reliable measurements, but sample transport and processing can take time. A simple field-screening assay could provide preliminary on-site information to support faster sampling, monitoring and follow-up decisions.

psychiatry

Potential applications

Agricultural runoff, lakes, ponds, irrigation systems, wastewater-affected water and bloom-risk monitoring.

info

Important limitation

Phosphate indicates nutrient loading and bloom potential. It does not directly identify algae, cyanobacteria or algal toxins, and laboratory confirmation may be needed.
